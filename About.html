Founders Story

Built for players who want more than gear.

Jason Naidoo • Est. 2023
Founder

Jason Naidoo

Jason Naidoo founded Next LvL Gaming in 2023 with a simple but ambitious idea: make premium gaming culture more accessible, more personal, and more community-driven. Before launching the brand, he spent years watching gamers spend heavily on equipment without receiving the same level of attention, service, and identity that real players deserve.

Origins

From late-night gaming sessions to a real brand

What began as a passion project during late-night sessions and local tournaments quickly grew into something bigger. Jason saw that competitive gamers, creators, and everyday enthusiasts all needed more than just products—they needed a space that understood their grind, their style, and their ambition.

Mission

Performance, identity, and community

Today, Next LvL Gaming is built around three values: premium performance, bold identity, and authentic community. Every product, partnership, and experience is shaped around the way gamers actually live and compete—whether they are building a setup at home or stepping onto the stage.

Our Promise
